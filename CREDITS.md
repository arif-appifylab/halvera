# Photo credits

The Halvera demo content uses 35 stock photos (33 from Pexels, 2 from Unsplash).
They are hotlinked from the source CDN, not stored in this repository. The machine-readable list, with
alt text and image URLs, is `src/data/media-credits.js`.

Licences. Both allow free use, including commercial use, without asking permission or paying, and
without attribution (credit is appreciated, so we give it here). Neither allows selling the photos
unaltered or implying that the people shown endorse a product.

- Pexels License: https://www.pexels.com/license/
- Unsplash License: https://unsplash.com/license

Every person shown is a man (client brief). Some topic photos show food or objects rather than people.
People in the photos are models, not Halvera's practitioners or clients. A school replaces
these with its own photos; without one, pages fall back to `public/images/hero-placeholder.svg` or
the component placeholder.
